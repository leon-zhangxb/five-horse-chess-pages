More Than a Game

Five Horse Chess (五馬棋) is a traditional board game of the Gelao people, played for generations in the mountainous regions of Guizhou, China. It was listed among the first batch of municipal intangible cultural heritage of Zunyi in 2006, and as provincial intangible cultural heritage of Guizhou in 2009.

Its board symbolizes five galloping horses, and each game mirrors the ancient arts of one-against-two strikes and group encirclement — a living reflection of the life and wisdom of the Gelao people.

五马棋 — 贵州仡佬族非遗棋类

五马棋是流传于贵州遵义正安、务川、湄潭等仡佬族地区的传统棋类，2006 年列入遵义市 第一批非物质文化遗产名录，2009 年列入贵州省第三批省级非物质文化遗产名录。

本应用将其完整数字化：四种对战模式、原汁原味的「挑」「夹」「复盘补子」规则、 16 种界面语言、完全离线、无广告无账号 — 让这门古老的民间棋艺在手机上重新 焕发生机。
