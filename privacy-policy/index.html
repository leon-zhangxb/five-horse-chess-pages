Privacy Policy

Five Horse Chess · Last updated: October 26, 2026

Five Horse Chess (the “App”) is developed by Wuhan Jindouzi Technology Co., Ltd (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

In short: the App does not collect or share any personal data. It has no account system and works fully offline — everything it saves stays locally on your device. The one optional exception is Game Center achievements, which are handled by Apple; see section 4.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Usage analytics, diagnostics, or crash logs
  • Advertising identifiers (such as IDFA)
  • Account credentials — the App has no account system of its own

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App saves the following data locally on your device only:

  • Game records (win / loss / draw counts per game mode)
  • Your interface language preference

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Deleting the App removes all of it. Note that achievement progress shown by Game Center is held by Apple, not stored by the App — see section 4.

3. Network Access

The App is fully functional offline. All four game modes, the AI opponent, and your local records work with no connection at all. Network access occurs in only two situations, both of them optional:

  • Reference links. When you tap a reference link on the “Heritage Intro” page, the App opens a public web page (for example, a Baidu Baike article) for you to read. When you visit such a third-party page, that website may process data under its own privacy policy. We do not control these websites and do not receive any data from them.
  • Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, the App reports achievement progress to Apple. See section 4. If you are not signed in, or your device is offline, this never happens and the App remains fully playable.

4. Game Center & Third-Party Services

Game Center (Apple Inc.). The App supports Game Center achievements as an optional, supplementary feature. It is not required in order to play: no game mode, difficulty level, or content is gated behind it, and declining to use it changes nothing about the game.

  • What is shared. Only your achievement progress (for example, “10 wins as Commander”) and the fact that you earned it. Your match records, win/loss statistics, and language preference are never uploaded.
  • Who receives it. Apple, through the Game Center service built into iOS. The Developer neither receives nor stores any of this data — we operate no server of our own.
  • How it is governed. Apple’s handling of Game Center data is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy. Your Game Center profile and friend list are likewise managed entirely by Apple; the App has no access to them.
  • How to avoid it. Simply do not sign in to Game Center, or sign out in iOS Settings. The App detects this and silently skips all achievement reporting — no error prompts, no nagging, no loss of functionality.
  • Deleting your achievement data. This data is held by Apple, not by us. Manage or delete it through your Apple ID, or contact Apple.

No other third-party services. Beyond the optional Game Center integration described above, the App integrates no third-party services of any kind, including but not limited to: analytics (e.g., Firebase), advertising (e.g., AdMob), crash reporting, social login, and payment services. StoreKit and in-app purchases are not used. The App shows no advertising.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region). It is safe for use by children and families. If a child uses Game Center, that is governed by the child’s Apple ID and Family Sharing settings, which are controlled by the parent or guardian.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because the App collects no personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, delete, or export. Any data the App stores locally can be removed entirely by deleting the App. Achievement data held by Apple can be managed through your Apple ID.

7. Data Security

All App data — your match records and language preference — is stored locally on your device and protected by iOS platform security. It is never transmitted to the Developer, because the Developer operates no server.

The only outbound data is optional Game Center achievement progress, which iOS sends directly to Apple over an encrypted connection. See section 4.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

隐私政策

五马棋（Five Horse Chess） · 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 26 日

五马棋（下称“本应用”）由 Wuhan Jindouzi Technology Co., Ltd（下称“我们”）开发。 本隐私政策说明本应用如何处理您的信息。

简而言之：本应用不收集、不共享任何个人数据。 应用没有账号系统，可完全离线运行，所有保存的数据仅留在您的设备本地。 唯一的可选例外是 Game Center 成就，该项由 Apple 处理，详见第 4 节。

1. 我们不收集的信息

  • 个人身份信息（姓名、邮箱、电话号码等）
  • 位置数据
  • 通讯录、照片、相机或麦克风数据
  • 使用分析、诊断或崩溃日志
  • 广告标识符（如 IDFA）
  • 账号凭证——本应用没有自有的账号系统

2. 仅存储于您设备上的信息

本应用仅在您的设备本地保存以下数据：

  • 对局战绩（各模式的胜 / 负 / 和统计）
  • 界面语言偏好

这些数据永远不会离开您的设备，我们也无法访问。删除本应用即会同时删除这些数据。 请注意：Game Center 显示的成就进度由 Apple 保管，本应用不做存储，详见第 4 节。

3. 网络访问

本应用可完全离线使用。四种玩法、AI 对手与本地战绩均无需联网即可运行。 网络访问仅发生在以下两种情形，且都是可选的：

  • 参考资料链接。当您主动点击“非遗介绍”页面中的参考资料链接时， 应用会打开公开网页（例如百度百科词条）供您阅读。当您访问此类第三方网页时， 该网站可能依据其自身的隐私政策处理数据。我们无法控制这些网站，也不会收到它们的任何数据。
  • Game Center。若您已在设备上登录 Game Center， 本应用会向 Apple 上报成就进度，详见第 4 节。若您未登录或设备处于离线状态， 这一过程不会发生，本应用仍可完整游玩。

4. Game Center 与第三方服务

Game Center（Apple Inc.）。本应用支持 Game Center 成就， 这是一项可选的附加功能，并非游玩所必需： 没有任何模式、难度或内容需要依赖它解锁，不使用它对游戏本身没有任何影响。

  • 共享的内容。仅包括您的成就进度（例如“以主将身份累计赢下 10 局”） 以及您已达成该成就这一事实。您的对局记录、胜负统计与语言偏好绝不会被上传。
  • 接收方。Apple，经由 iOS 内置的 Game Center 服务。 开发者既不接收也不存储这些数据——我们不运营任何自有服务器。
  • 适用规则。Apple 对 Game Center 数据的处理适用 Apple 隐私政策。 您的 Game Center 个人资料与好友列表同样完全由 Apple 管理，本应用无权访问。
  • 如何避免。不登录 Game Center 即可，或在 iOS“设置”中退出登录。 本应用会检测到该状态并静默跳过全部成就上报——不弹窗、不提示、不影响任何功能。
  • 删除成就数据。该数据由 Apple 保管，不由我们保管。 请通过您的 Apple ID 管理或删除，或联系 Apple。

无其他第三方服务。除上述可选的 Game Center 集成外， 本应用未集成任何第三方服务，包括但不限于：数据分析（如 Firebase）、 广告（如 AdMob）、崩溃上报、社交登录与支付服务。本应用不使用 StoreKit，也不含内购， 且不展示任何广告。

5. 儿童隐私

本应用不向任何人（包括 13 周岁以下儿童）收集任何个人信息，适合儿童与家庭使用。 如儿童使用 Game Center，相关行为受该儿童的 Apple ID 与“家人共享”设置约束， 这些均由家长或监护人控制。

6. 您的权利（GDPR / CCPA）

由于本应用不收集任何个人数据，我们没有任何个人数据可供访问、更正、删除或导出。 应用本地存储的数据可通过删除本应用完全移除。由 Apple 保管的成就数据可通过您的 Apple ID 管理。

7. 数据安全

本应用的所有数据——对局战绩与语言偏好——均存储在您设备本地， 受 iOS 平台安全机制保护，不会传输给开发者，因为我们不运营任何服务器。

唯一的外发数据是可选的 Game Center 成就进度，由 iOS 通过加密连接直接发送给 Apple，详见第 4 节。

8. 政策变更

我们可能不时更新本隐私政策。最新版本将始终发布在本页面，并同步更新“最后更新”日期。
