Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start playing?

From the home screen, tap one of the four game-mode cards. In a game, tap one of your pieces to select it, then tap a target square to move it.

Where can I read the rules of the current mode?

In a game, tap the “?” button at the top-right of the navigation bar. The rules popover explains everything for that mode, including win conditions.

How do I switch the interface language?

Home screen → More → Language. Choose from 16 languages; the change takes effect immediately.

Where are my battle records stored?

Locally on your device only (Home → More → Battle Records). We have no access to them, and they are removed permanently when you delete the app.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. The game works fully offline. The only time the app accesses the network is when you explicitly tap a reference link on the Heritage Intro page, which opens a public webpage (for example, a Baidu Baike article) for you to read.

Do I need an account or login?

No. The app has no account system, no ads, and no in-app purchases.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your device. All locally stored data (battle records and language preference) is removed together with the app.

What are the system requirements?

iOS 16.0 or later. The app supports iPhone and iPad, in both portrait and landscape orientation.

Can two people play on one device?

Yes. “Commander Breakout” and “Local 2-Player” are designed for two players sharing a single device.

常见问题

如何开始游戏？

在首页点击任意一个游戏模式卡片进入对局。对局中，先点击己方棋子选中它， 再点击目标格即可落子。

在哪里查看当前模式的规则？

对局中点击导航栏右上角的“?”按钮，弹出的规则说明会解释该模式的 全部规则，包括胜负条件。

如何切换界面语言？

首页 → 更多 → 语言切换。可从 16 种语言中选择，切换后立即生效。

我的战绩保存在哪里？

仅保存在您的设备本地（首页 → 更多 → 个人战绩）。我们无法访问这些 数据；删除应用后战绩会被永久清除。

应用需要联网吗？

不需要。游戏可完全离线运行。唯一的联网场景是您主动点击“非遗介绍” 页面中的参考资料链接时，应用会打开公开网页（例如百度百科词条）供您阅读。

需要注册账号或登录吗？

不需要。本应用没有账号系统，没有广告，也不含内购。

如何删除我的数据？

从设备上删除本应用即可。所有本地存储的数据（对局战绩与语言偏好）会随应用 一并移除。

系统要求是什么？

需要 iOS 16.0 或更高版本。支持 iPhone 与 iPad，支持竖屏与横屏。

两个人可以在一台设备上对战吗？

可以。“主将突围”与“双人本地”模式正是为两位玩家 共用一台设备而设计。
